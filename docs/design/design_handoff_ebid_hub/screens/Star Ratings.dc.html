How your reputation is built, what raises and lowers it, and what to do if it falls.

WHY THIS EXISTS

A trust signal between strangers

Traders and Market Makers on ADWITIX often transact with someone they've never met, sometimes without seeing the item in person. Your Star Rating is how the platform — and everyone you deal with — knows you've genuinely honoured your commitments before. It's the mechanism that lets us apply lighter rules to trustworthy accounts and tighter ones to risky accounts.

TWO SEPARATE SCORES

Your Trader★ and Market Maker★ never mix

If you both trade and sell, you have two entirely independent scores. Both start at 3.0 out of 5.0 once your profile is complete, and neither moves just from inactivity — only real behaviour affects the number.

IF YOU DISAGREE

How to appeal a downgrade

A meaningful downgrade is never applied automatically — it's reviewed first, and always comes with a stated reason. If you believe a rating change was wrong:

  1. Check the stated reason on your Rating History — every change references the specific event behind it.
  2. Raise an appeal directly against that event, with any supporting evidence.
  3. Your appeal is reviewed once, by an authority independent of the original decision — the Custodian if a TSX Master decided it — and you'll receive a reasoned outcome either way.

For how disputes and appeals actually work, see our Dispute Resolution Process.
