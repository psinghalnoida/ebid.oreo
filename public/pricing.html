01 — Who's on the floor

Five roles. One ledger.

Every action on TradeSphereX is tied to a role, and every role has exactly the authority it needs — no more.

Symbol
Role
What they do
TSX
TradeSphereX
A tenant's own branded marketplace — a bank, insurer, NBFC, PSU, or firm's dedicated storefront on the platform.
TSXM
TSX Master
The tenant's administrator. Approves Market Makers, reviews every Lot, and sets local pricing within platform limits.
MM
Market Maker
An approved seller. Pushes Lots and Trading Sessions to market — solo, or through the API.
TRD
Trader
A verified buyer. Bids, offers, and settles trades across every TradeSphereX on the network.
CUS
Custodian
The platform's sovereign governance role. Whitelists TSX operators and audits the ledger — never trades, never lists. Fair to every party, answerable to none of them.
